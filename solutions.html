The ecosystem

Eight products, one connected record.

Every Wellinovate product reads and writes to the same patient record — so identity, care, diagnostics and payments move as one system instead of ten disconnected logins.

Flagship

welliRecord

A patient-centered digital health record that follows the person, not the provider — every visit, result and prescription in one place, shareable with a consent, never a fax.

  • One record, shared with consent across every connected provider
  • Structured history — visits, results and prescriptions in one timeline
  • Built on the same identity layer as the rest of the ecosystem
