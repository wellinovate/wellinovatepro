Who we are

Healthcare expertise, technology and design — at the same table.

Wellinovate was founded on one conviction: that the tools healthcare runs on deserved to be rebuilt by people who had actually worked inside the system.

Est. 2019Lagos · London · RemoteHealthcare-firstPrivacy by design

We start with the problem

Before any product is a product, it is a problem we've sat with — inside clinics, alongside patients, in the gaps between systems that were never meant to talk to each other. We design from there, not from a feature list.

Healthcare expertise, in-house

Clinicians, technologists, AI researchers and designers work the same table. Every product carries a working knowledge of how care is actually delivered — not a guess at it from outside.

Built for the whole system

Patients, providers, pharmacies and payers all touch the same records. We build for that whole system at once, so one login and one identity carry across every point of care.

Our team

People who've worked inside the system

Founded by clinicians and engineers who spent years inside hospitals and health ministries before deciding the tools deserved to be rebuilt.
