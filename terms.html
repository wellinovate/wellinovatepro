2. Clinical Decision Support & Physician Responsibility

Crucial Medical Notice: Wellinovate platforms provide digital health infrastructure, clinical workflow automation, and algorithmic decision support. Wellinovate does not practice medicine. The ultimate responsibility for patient assessment, clinical diagnosis, therapeutic judgment, prescription authorization, and patient management rests exclusively with licensed healthcare practitioners.

Algorithms, risk scoring, drug interaction flags, and predictive alerts surfaced within WelliMate and WelliDiagnostic are assistive tools designed to augment clinician situational awareness. Clinicians must independently verify all algorithmic suggestions against their own physical examination, diagnostic judgment, and institutional standard operating procedures.

3. Authorized Access, Credentials & welliID

Institutional customers must ensure that all accounts provisioned for clinical personnel are authenticated using verified identities:

  • Practitioner Credentialing: Facilities must restrict clinical charting, prescription writing, and chart closure privileges exclusively to credentialed, actively licensed medical personnel.
  • Multi-Factor Authentication: Account credentials and hardware security tokens must never be shared across shifts or individuals. Every digital signature is cryptographically bound to an individual practitioner profile via welliID.
  • Immediate Revocation: Facilities must immediately revoke system access credentials upon departure, suspension, or change of role of any clinical or administrative personnel.

4. Service Level Commitments & Offline Continuity

Healthcare systems demand uncompromised reliability. Wellinovate commits to:

  • Target Availability: A 99.95% monthly uptime target for core cloud routing and synchronization services, excluding scheduled preventive maintenance windows announced at least 7 days in advance.
  • Offline Resilience: In primary care clinics and secondary facilities with unstable grid connectivity, local Wellinovate edge units continue operating autonomously, storing consult notes, intake vitals, and diagnostic reads in local encrypted queues that sync atomically upon connection recovery.
  • Incident Severity Protocol: Critical Sev-1 incidents impacting active patient triage or emergency care workflows trigger 24/7 priority emergency response within 15 minutes.

5. Intellectual Property & Clinical Data Ownership

  • Wellinovate Technology: The underlying algorithms, user interface designs, design system tokens, firmware, neural models, documentation, and trade secrets remain the exclusive intellectual property of Wellinovate Ltd. and its licensors.

6. Acceptable Use & Patient Safety Safeguards
